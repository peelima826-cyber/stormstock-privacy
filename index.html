Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

The short version

  • StormStock helps neighbors track essential supplies and hazards before and after a hurricane.
  • You can use the app anonymously — signing in is optional.
  • We use your location only to show nearby stores, alerts, and your neighborhood — you can turn it off.
  • Reports, chat messages, and help posts you submit are shared publicly with your community.
  • We do not sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • Photos you attach expire automatically after 24 hours.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the StormStock mobile app ("StormStock", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using StormStock, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Location

With your permission, we use your device's approximate and precise location to: find stores and hazards near you, determine your neighborhood for the community chat and help board, alert you when a hazard or storm is near, and target safety notifications. You can deny or revoke location access in your device settings; some features (finding nearby supplies, local alerts) will be limited without it.

Optional account

Signing in is optional and only unlocks a verified name and family/friends features. If you sign in with Google, our authentication provider (Clerk) receives your name and email address to create your account. You can use the entire app without an account.

Device & notification data

If you enable push notifications, we store a push token for your device (so we can deliver safety alerts) along with your approximate location/neighborhood and a random app-generated ID. This random ID is not linked to your real identity unless you choose to sign in.

Content you share

Information you submit — stock reports, "open/closed" reports, price reports, hazard reports, chat messages, help posts, group messages, a display name you choose, and your safe/needs-help status — is stored on our servers and, except for direct messages and groups, is visible to other users in your area. Stock and hazard reports are collected anonymously by design.

Photos

Photos you attach to a hazard or chat are uploaded to a third-party image host (imgbb) and are set to expire after 24 hours. Photos are screened by an automated moderation step before posting. Damage-documentation photos you take for insurance are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

AI assistant

When you use the in-app assistant, the messages you send are transmitted to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a response. Don't share sensitive personal information with the assistant.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide the core features: finding nearby supplies, mapping hazards, community chat and mutual aid.
  • Send safety notifications (nearby hazard, a friend's SOS, tornado warnings, new storms, messages).
  • Show official weather, storm, and shelter information for your area.
  • Maintain community trust (contribution levels, agreement counts, basic moderation).
  • Keep the service working, secure, and free of abuse.

4. Public content

StormStock is a community tool. Reports, chat messages, help posts, and any display name or status you share may be seen by other people in your region and can be quoted or screenshotted by them. Please don't post anything you wouldn't want your neighbors to see, and don't share exact home addresses or other sensitive details.

5. How long we keep information

  • Attached photos: expire automatically after 24 hours.
  • Reports, messages, and help posts: kept to provide the historical community record; older stock reports are hidden from the app after 24 hours.
  • Damage-documentation photos: stored only on your device until you delete them.
  • Account data: kept while your account exists.

6. Your choices

  • Use anonymously: the app works without an account.
  • Location: allow or deny it in your device settings at any time.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in the app's Prep screen or device settings.
  • Sign out / delete: sign out in the app; to delete your account or request deletion of data associated with you, email us at the address below.

7. Children

StormStock is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including restricted database access and secure connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes may be highlighted in the app.

Adaptive by design

The app changes as the storm does.

StormStock reads live data from the National Weather Service and the National Hurricane Center, then reshapes itself around what you need at that exact moment.

01 · Calm

Build your kit, check the tropics, find what's nearby. No panic, no noise.

02 · Threat

A watch or the cone reaches you. Supplies and evacuation zones move front and center.

03 · Impact

Tornado warning or landfall. Everything collapses into shelter, SOS, and family.

04 · Recovery

What's open, where the flooding and downed lines are, and documenting damage.

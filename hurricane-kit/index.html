Hurricane supply kit

Most kit lists are written as if the power comes back the same evening. Build for three days minimum, a week if you can, and assume no refrigeration and no card readers.

Water and food

  • One gallon of water per person per day. Three days minimum, seven if you have the space.
  • Extra water for pets, and for washing.
  • Food that needs no cooking, no water and no refrigeration.
  • A manual can opener — the single most forgotten item on this list.
  • Coolers and as much ice as you can hold. Ice is the first thing to disappear and the last to come back.

Power and light

  • Flashlights and headlamps, plus spare batteries in the right sizes.
  • A battery or hand-crank radio, for when the network is down.
  • Portable phone batteries, charged before the storm.
  • Candles are a last resort, not a plan — open flame in a dark house full of debris is how fires start.

Health and documents

  • Prescriptions filled for at least a week.
  • A first aid kit, and any medical equipment that needs power planned for separately.
  • Copies of insurance policies, IDs and the deed or lease, reachable without internet.
  • Cash in small bills.

The house

  • Tarps, rope and duct tape for temporary repairs.
  • A wrench for the gas shutoff if you have gas service.
  • Trash bags, wet wipes and hand sanitiser for when water is limited.
  • Bug spray. Standing water after a storm means mosquitoes within days.

Common questions

How many days should a hurricane kit cover?

Three days is the standard minimum and seven is more realistic for a major storm, because restoring power to an entire county takes longer than restoring it to one street.

What is the most commonly forgotten item?

A manual can opener, closely followed by cash in small bills and pet supplies. All three become impossible to obtain once the storm is close.

Do I need a generator?

Only if someone in the household depends on powered medical equipment, or if you can store and run it safely outdoors. A generator used indoors is more dangerous than the outage it solves.
